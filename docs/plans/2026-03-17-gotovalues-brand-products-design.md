# gotovalues Brand And Products Design

## Approved Direction

- Rebrand the current app from `GoToValues` to `gotovalues` everywhere in the active Next.js app and supporting docs.
- Keep the sender address `hello@send.gotovalues.com`, but change the display name to `gotovalues`.
- Move `/produkty` away from the current heavy editorial feel toward a calmer product-first presentation.

## Visual Thesis

The current page is readable, but the relationship between hero typography and the cards is off. The display face dominates too much, so the portfolio itself feels secondary. The approved correction is not a redesign from scratch. It is a rebalancing:

- make the brand mark more product-like and less ceremonial,
- shorten and soften the products hero,
- reduce heading scale globally,
- strengthen product cards so they carry more of the page's visual weight,
- slightly raise contrast for supporting text and tags.

## Scope

- Update current app brand strings, metadata, mail subject/body text, and public docs.
- Refine `/produkty` hero copy and supporting section headings.
- Adjust shared typography and product card styling in `src/app/globals.css`.
- Leave archived legacy files as reference material unless they affect the active app flow.
