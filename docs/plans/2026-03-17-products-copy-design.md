# gotovalues Products Copy Design

## Goal

Turn `/produkty` from a neutral portfolio page into a sales-supporting proof page for operational clients.

## Direction

The page should answer a practical buyer question:

> "Czy ten człowiek naprawdę umie zbudować narzędzie pod proces, dokumenty i dane?"

That means the copy should:

- frame public products as proof of execution, not just portfolio pieces,
- frame private implementations as evidence of work with document-heavy and operational workflows,
- stay honest about what is public and what is not,
- avoid sounding like a generic creative portfolio.

## Narrative Order

1. Explain why this page exists
2. Show public proof
3. Show private-but-relevant implementation categories
4. Translate each example into business capability
