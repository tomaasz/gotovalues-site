# Requirements

## REQ-about-trust
- source: docs/plans/2026-03-17-about-trust-design.md
- description: Strengthen the homepage About section with professional identity, operational and domain experience, and a direct collaboration style while preserving gotovalues' process-focused positioning.
- acceptance: The section identifies Tomasz Gołaszewski, shows experience with processes, assets, data, and tools, reads as a credible operational partner, and remains short enough for the lightweight homepage.
- scope: homepage About section, professional identity, domain experience, operational consulting, trust points

## REQ-contact-form-friction
- source: docs/plans/2026-03-17-contact-form-cro-design.md
- description: Increase homepage contact submissions by reducing form friction and letting visitors describe a concrete operational problem in their own language.
- acceptance: The form contains first name, email, optional company, and one main message field, removes the service classification step, uses low-friction helper and success copy, and produces a simpler email payload.
- scope: homepage contact form, fields, microcopy, conversion

## REQ-brand-products
- source: docs/plans/2026-03-17-gotovalues-brand-products-design.md
- description: Apply the lowercase gotovalues brand across the active app and supporting docs while moving the products page toward a calmer product-first presentation.
- acceptance: Active brand strings, metadata, mail text, and public docs use gotovalues, the products hero is shorter and calmer, heading scale is reduced, product cards carry more visual weight, and supporting text has adequate contrast.
- scope: gotovalues brand, products page, metadata, typography, product cards

## REQ-homepage-conversion
- source: docs/plans/2026-03-17-homepage-conversion-copy-design.md
- description: Improve homepage copy for operational and production-oriented customer acquisition without changing the page structure.
- acceptance: Hero, offer, proof, About, contact, and form copy answer whether the offer fits the visitor's problem, demonstrate process understanding, and invite a conversation about one concrete problem without unverified claims.
- scope: homepage copy, customer acquisition, operational pain, proof, contact

## REQ-homepage-positioning
- source: docs/plans/2026-03-17-homepage-copy-design.md
- description: Position the homepage for local production and processing companies that need custom web applications and practical AI tools for real operational processes.
- acceptance: Messaging follows the order pain recognition, solution type, practical working style, real-product credibility, and one-process conversation, with direct, practical, anti-buzzword, business-first tone and AI subordinate to the business problem.
- scope: homepage, production and processing companies, custom tools, practical AI, operational processes

## REQ-production-landing
- source: docs/plans/2026-03-17-production-landing-design.md
- description: Create a focused `/dla-produkcji` landing page for cold outreach to production and processing companies with manual document, quality, and exception workflows.
- acceptance: The page addresses production-specific symptoms, presents lightweight tools without promising ERP replacement, uses public and private work as proof, explains a small first step, and has one short-contact CTA.
- scope: /dla-produkcji, production, processing, documents, quality, workflow, exceptions, cold outreach

## REQ-products-proof
- source: docs/plans/2026-03-17-products-copy-design.md
- description: Turn `/produkty` into a sales-supporting proof page for buyers evaluating custom tools, workflow systems, and practical AI implementations.
- acceptance: The page explains its purpose, shows public proof, describes private implementation categories honestly, and translates examples into business capabilities without generic portfolio language.
- scope: /produkty, public products, private implementations, operational workflows, business capabilities
