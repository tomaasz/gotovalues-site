# Requirements: gotovalues-site

**Defined:** 2026-11-25
**Core Value:** Public visitors can quickly understand how gotovalues helps with operational problems and can send a low-friction contact request.

## v1 Requirements

### Homepage Trust and Positioning

- [ ] **REQ-about-trust**: The homepage About section identifies Tomasz Gołaszewski, shows relevant process, asset, data, and tool experience, and reads as a credible operational partner while staying concise.
- [ ] **REQ-homepage-positioning**: Homepage messaging targets local production and processing companies needing custom web applications and practical AI for operational processes, using a direct business-first tone.
- [ ] **REQ-homepage-conversion**: Hero, offer, proof, About, contact, and form copy explain fit, demonstrate process understanding, and invite a conversation about one concrete problem without unverified claims.

### Contact Conversion

- [ ] **REQ-contact-form-friction**: The contact form uses first name, email, optional company, and one main message field with low-friction helper and success copy and a simple email payload.

### Brand and Product Proof

- [ ] **REQ-brand-products**: Active brand strings, metadata, mail text, and public docs use lowercase gotovalues, while `/produkty` uses calmer copy, stronger product cards, reduced heading scale, and adequate contrast.
- [ ] **REQ-products-proof**: `/produkty` explains its purpose, shows public proof, describes private implementation categories honestly, and translates examples into business capabilities.

### Production Acquisition

- [ ] **REQ-production-landing**: `/dla-produkcji` addresses production-specific document, quality, and exception workflows, presents lightweight tools without promising ERP replacement, shows honest proof, explains a small first step, and has one short-contact CTA.

| Feature | Reason |

| Unverified claims | Copy must remain evidence-based and practical. |
